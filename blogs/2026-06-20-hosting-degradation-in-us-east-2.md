Jun 20 , 19:11 UTC Resolved - The issue related to hosting services in our US-East-2 Region has now been resolved. At this time, all of the reported symptoms should now be resolved: - Inability of some virtual machines in this region to change state (ie, run, suspend, shutdown) - Inability of some running virtual machines in this region to be saved as a template, copied or deleted This disruption may have resulted in some running Virtual Machines being returned to a powered off state. If you have a VM reporting an error or issue, please contact Kyndryl Cloud Uplift Support (support@skytap.com)
